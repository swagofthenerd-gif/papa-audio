# What's new in Papa Audio

Here's what has landed recently, in plain English. No jargon — just what changed
for you.

## Movies & Anime

- Movies, TV and anime now live right inside Papa Audio — browse, search and
  play them without leaving the app.
- Search understands what you mean: type a title naturally and it finds it, even
  with the odd typo or extra word.
- Every title has a proper detail page with genres you can click, the cast, and a
  note of what you've already watched.
- A "Because you watched…" shelf on Home suggests things based on what you've
  actually enjoyed.
- Person pages let you tap an actor or director and see everything they're in.
- Big anime and TV seasons load smoothly now, even when there are hundreds of
  episodes — the episode grid only draws what's on screen.
- Ratings from IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic are pulled in where
  available, so you can judge a title at a glance.
- "Surprise me" picks something for you when you can't decide.
- A "Find soundtrack" button on any film or show jumps straight to a music
  search for its score.
- The diary now has a timeline: your film and TV viewings woven together with
  the first time you heard each album, grouped by month.

## Player

- Click the picture to pause, scroll to change the volume, and see clear status
  messages (buffering, volume) right on the video.
- Chapter marks show on the seek bar, and the part that hasn't downloaded yet is
  shown as a distinct hatched region so you know how far you can jump.
- Skip Intro and Skip Credits appear automatically for shows that have them.
- Subtitles: pick from ones bundled with the download or search online, adjust
  their size and background, and the app remembers your timing tweaks per show.
- "Start over" or resume where you left off — your choice — and it remembers your
  volume and mute settings between sessions.
- A stats panel shows real download speed, connected peers and progress while you
  watch.
- If a source keeps stalling, the app quietly switches to a better one and keeps
  your place — no restart, no fuss.
- Auto-play-next, an "Up Next" preview, and a gentle "Still watching?" check so a
  series doesn't run all night by itself.
- Video starts faster: the player warms up at the same time the download begins
  instead of waiting for it.
- A camera button in the player (or Shift+S) grabs a screenshot of the current
  frame and saves it to your Pictures folder.
- Skip an intro by hand twice in a season and the app offers to skip it for you
  from then on — it learns the timing from your own jumps.

## Sources

- Far wider reach: the app now searches dozens of sources at once and races the
  fastest mirrors, so titles that used to come up empty now play.
- Healthy sources are remembered and tried first, and flaky ones are never
  dropped entirely — just moved down the list.
- You can pre-download the next episode, cap how much bandwidth streaming uses,
  and choose whether to keep seeding after you're done.
- If a source stops working you can edit its mirror list under Settings →
  Video → Advanced, so you're never stuck waiting for a fix.
- Pick a particular source for a show and keep watching it, and the app
  remembers your choice for the next episode — clear it any time from the
  sources list.

## Music

- A duplicate finder spots repeated tracks in your library.
- Better queue tools for building and reordering what plays next.
- Listening stats with a chart of what you've been playing.
- A sleep timer to fade the music out and stop after a set time.

## Under the hood

- Your watch history and settings are kept in a crash-proof store, so a sudden
  shutdown no longer loses where you were.
- Export and import your entire setup to a single file, and restore it later.
- Automatic backups run quietly in the background and keep the last several, so
  there's always a recent copy to fall back on.
- If the app ever hits a problem, it writes a plain-English note to a crash log
  you can send to the developer — no passwords or personal data in it.
- The app notices when your internet drops and lets you know, rather than
  silently failing.
- A health chip, a diagnostics page, a settings search box, and a one-click reset
  make it easier to see what's going on and fix it.
