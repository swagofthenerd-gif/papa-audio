      Playback
      Shared: the system mixer resamples to its own rate; other apps keep their sound. Exclusive device access: Papa Audio opens the device alone at the file’s own rate — nothing else can play through it while a track is loaded, and if the device refuses, playback falls back to the system output and says so here. This is not bit-perfect on its own — EQ, ReplayGain, volume leveling, the volume boost and crossfade all still change the samples. Use Bit-perfect mode below to turn those off.
      Uses the ReplayGain tags in your files; a file without them plays unchanged, so a mixed library can still jump in volume. Changes apply to what is playing without restarting it. This is not a sample-exact path — the BIT-PERFECT badge turns off while it is on.
      Gain policy: loading…
      Unplugging headphones or a DAC mid-song: Pause keeps your place and waits; Keep playing switches to whatever is now the default output — usually the speakers — at the current volume.
      Opens the device exclusively AND turns off EQ, ReplayGain, volume leveling, the volume boost and crossfade, so the file’s own samples reach the DAC. Gapless stays on, and a queue that mixes sample rates is still resampled at the handoff to keep it seamless — that is a deliberate choice in favour of not hearing a gap between tracks.
      Uses the loudness scan from the Stats page to level quiet and loud tracks at playback. Nothing is written to your files. Scan first, then turn this on.

      Sharing (seeding) sends the parts you already have back to other people watching the same thing. It keeps things fast for everyone and is on by default.
      Who receives what when you use Movies & TV
      • TMDB and AniList get the titles you search and open, to show posters, episodes and details.
      • Torrent indexers (Nyaa, AnimeTosho, YTS, EZTV, SolidTorrents, Knaben, apibay, and Jackett if you set one up) get the title, year and episode you ask for.
      • RealDebrid, if a token is set, gets the magnet links of the sources you play.
      • Other torrent users can see your IP address while a torrent streams. While "Share while watching" is on they also download from you; off, you only download.
      • Never sent: your watch history, lists, ratings, notes, or anything about your music. Support bundles are scrubbed of tokens and paths.
      Anime is made at 1080p or less, so on a bigger screen something has to stretch it. This redraws the outlines instead of blurring them. It needs the Purist player above, and changing it takes effect straight away — you do not have to restart the episode.
      Advanced: source mirrors
      One address per line, or comma-separated. Leave a field empty to use the built-in mirrors. Only change these if a source has stopped working.
      Debrid account
      With an account, matching torrents stream over plain HTTP instead of P2P — faster and no seeding. Get a token from your RealDebrid account page.
